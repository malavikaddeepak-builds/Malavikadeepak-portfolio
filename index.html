About Me

Where economics
meets ambition

I come from a pure economics background — but my first job threw me straight into the world of business development at an IT and AI company. That collision of two worlds didn't confuse me — it ignited me. I discovered a deep love for tech while never letting go of my passion for economics, personal development, and books.

Today I channel all of it: I build BDE Workspace, a live product designed to help business development professionals prioritise their work and tasks. I share daily vlogs and book reviews on Instagram. I write about growth, business, and everyday life on Medium. And I show up on LinkedIn with real stories, lessons, and advice from the field.

My goal is to take everything I have built — hands-on experience, self-taught tech skills, and a genuine obsession with growth — into a top MBA programme and come out ready to lead, build, and give back.
